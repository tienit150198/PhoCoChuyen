## Shared work limits and what they mean

`game/engine.py:288-307` sets MORE_ACTIVE=4 unfinished jobs (including deferred) and MORE_DAY=12 generated jobs/day. `more_work` calls this gate at `:770-778`. This is an implemented work-in-progress limit for all normal career-task admission; it is not an owned shelf, room, machine or medical staffing ratio. A capacity entitlement can change this gate meaningfully, with matching UI and admission paths; it should be named as active-work slots and not invented office equipment. Food-service spontaneous arrivals separately hardcode4 (`game/careers/food_service.py:212-213`). Preserve day/budget earnings controls and specialized shift restrictions. Save80 tasks and journal history sizes are serialization limits, not things to sell.
