About `ting.mp3`: a real pet-training (dome) bell recorded with a microphone, not a synthesised tone and not any bank's notification sound. It was cut from the source's HQ preview (the leading silence removed, 1.1 s kept with a 0.5 s fade-out), normalised to −3 dBFS peak and re-encoded to MP3 (mono, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps, no tags). The pitch was not changed.

About `horn.mp3`: a real car horn recorded in traffic (a field recording, not a synthesised tone). It was cut from the source's HQ preview (1.20 s → 2.05 s: a short honk and the start of a longer one, 20 ms fade-in, 180 ms fade-out), normalised to −3 dBFS peak and re-encoded to MP3 (mono, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps, no tags). The pitch was not changed. Its licence (Creative Commons 0, not a remix) was checked on the source page on 2026-10-17. A bicycle's horn is the recorded bell above (`ting.mp3`).

The licence was checked on the source page on 2026-10-02. `public/js/v4/sounds.js` plays it (through `public/js/audio.js` `loadSample` / `Sound.ting`).
