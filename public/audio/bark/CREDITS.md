# Dog barks (🐕 Kéo co chó sủa)

Seven short recordings of real dogs barking, played by `public/js/v4/dog-bark.js` when the opponent's dog barks in the
tug-of-war. Every file here is released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** on Freesound.org:
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. It may be used, changed and shared without asking and without
credit. We credit the authors anyway.

Each file was cut from the source's HQ preview: the leading and trailing silence removed (one bark kept, 0.17–0.34 s),
normalised to −3 dBFS peak, an 80 ms fade-out, re-encoded to MP3 (mono, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps, no tags). The pitch was
not changed in the files; the page plays each bark at a rate, length and loudness of its own per breed, so no two
barks sound the same. The licences were checked on the source pages on 2026-10-10.
